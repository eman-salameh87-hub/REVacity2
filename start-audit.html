Where is the money going?

Revenue Audit

A forensic examination of your revenue topology — pricing architecture, margin structure, promotional impact, channel economics, and demand patterns. We find the leaks you didn't know existed.

  • Pricing elasticity analysis

Why aren't you growing?

Growth Audit

A structural analysis of your growth system — acquisition loops, retention mechanics, expansion pathways, market positioning, and competitive dynamics. We find where momentum is stuck.

Deliverables

  • Growth system map
  • Acquisition loop analysis
  • Retention mechanics review
  • Market positioning assessment
  • Growth engineering roadmap

What does your brand actually mean?

Cognitive Audit

An examination of what your brand means in the mind of your market. We identify gaps between intended meaning and perceived meaning — and the revenue impact of that gap.

Deliverables

  • Perception vs. reality map

Where is legacy dragging you?

Modernization Audit

A technical and operational assessment of your systems, processes, and tools. We identify where legacy infrastructure is creating friction, cost, or competitive disadvantage.

Deliverables

  • Modernization roadmap

What makes our audits different.

Most agency "audits" are sales tools. They find just enough problems to justify a retainer, but not enough to actually solve anything. Ours are the opposite.

We audit to expose the full picture — even the parts that don't lead to an engagement with us. If the problem is your team, we'll say so. If the problem is your product, we'll say so. If the problem doesn't exist, we'll say that too.

The diagnostic is the first test of trust. If we can't be honest here, why would you trust us with your revenue?
